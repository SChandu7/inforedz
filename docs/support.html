Delete your account

  1. Open the InfoREDZ app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Your account, profile, location, blood stock records, donation history and requests are deleted permanently from both the app and the website. If you can't sign in, email infusionmedzone@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my InfoREDZ account" and we will delete it within 7 days.

Report a profile

If a contributor or centre profile is fake, wrong or abusive:

  • Contributor: open their details and tap Report. Tap Hide to stop seeing them.
  • Centre: open the centre and use the ⋮ menu to report or hide it.

Reports come to our team by email. We review them and remove profiles that break our terms.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Continue as Guest to browse contributors and centres. You need an account to see contributors' phone numbers and to register as a contributor or centre.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We send a 6-digit code that is valid for 10 minutes.

How do I stop people contacting me?

In your profile, turn off Available. You won't appear on the map or in "available" searches. Delete your account to remove your profile completely.

Who can register as a contributor?

People aged 18–65 who weigh at least 45 kg and are in good health. Always confirm your eligibility with the blood centre before donating.

Does InfoREDZ give medical advice?

No. InfoREDZ helps people connect. Follow the guidance of your doctor and the blood centre.
