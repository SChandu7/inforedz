Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 October 2026 · Applies to the InfoREDZ apps for iPhone and Android and the InfoREDZ website.

InfoREDZ ("we", "us") connects voluntary blood donors ("contributors"), people who need blood, and blood banks ("centres") in India. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

1. What we collect

When you create an account

  • All accounts: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), account type (contributor or centre), city.
  • Contributors: phone number, blood group, age, gender, weight, when you last donated, whether you have a health condition that affects donating, your availability, and the number of donations you log.
  • Centres: centre name, address, phone number, opening hours, and blood stock levels.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your precise location while the app is open to show contributors and centres near you and to fill in your city when you register. If you save it to your profile, your coordinates are stored so you can appear on the map. The app never reads your location in the background. You can enter a city by hand instead, and you can turn location off at any time in your phone's settings.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report (the error, device model, operating system version and app version) to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. Crash reports are not linked to your account.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, photos, messages, browsing history, advertising identifier, or payment details.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account and let you sign in, including sending password-reset codes to your email.
  • To let people who need blood find and contact available contributors and centres.
  • To show contributors and centres on the map and in search results.
  • To show overall counts (for example, number of contributors and cities).
  • To keep the service safe, investigate reports of fake or abusive profiles, and fix crashes.

3. Who can see your information

  • Anyone using the app or website, including guests: a contributor's name, blood group, city, age, gender, weight, availability, last-donated period and donation count; and a centre's full details. Available contributors who saved a location appear as a pin on the map.
  • Signed-in users only: contributors' phone numbers, so they can call or WhatsApp you, and a contributor's email address if they have not added a phone number.
  • Never visible to anyone, including us: your password.

Only register as a contributor if you are comfortable being contacted about blood donation. You can mark yourself as unavailable at any time in your profile.

4. Services we use

We share only what each service needs to work:

  • Our server (api.chandus7.in) stores account data for the app and the website.
  • Google Maps displays maps. Google receives map requests from your device.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim turns your coordinates into a city name, and Photon (komoot) suggests cities as you type. They receive the coordinates or search text, not your account details.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives crash reports, which it keeps for 90 days.
  • Email delivery sends password-reset codes to your email address.
  • Phone and WhatsApp: when you tap Call or WhatsApp, your phone's own app opens, and that app's privacy policy applies.

We may disclose information if Indian law requires it.

5. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Password-reset codes expire after 10 minutes.

To delete your account: open the app, go to Profile → ⋮ menu → Delete account and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes your account, profile, location, blood stock records, donation history and requests from our server, for both the app and the website. If you can't sign in, email us from your registered email address (see Support) and we will delete it within 7 days.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can access and correct your data (most of it directly in your profile), withdraw consent by deleting your account, and raise a grievance with us. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within 7 days.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and your sign-in session token is kept in your phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

8. Age

Contributors must be between 18 and 65 years old to register. InfoREDZ is not directed at children under 18.

9. Medical disclaimer

InfoREDZ helps people connect; it does not provide medical advice. Always follow the guidance of your doctor and the blood centre.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app for significant changes.
